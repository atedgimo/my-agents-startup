# Objectives and Key Results (OKRs)

## Objective 1: Establish MVP Market Foundation
**Description:** Create the core infrastructure that allows a local artisan to go live with their marketing and customer service automated systems.

*   **KR 1:** Successfully integrate an Automated Marketing Engine capable of generating 30 days of content in <15 minutes.
*   **KR 2:** Build a Customer Support Hub prototype that can handle 80% of standard local business FAQs (pricing, hours, availability).
*   **KR 3:** Implement basic Inventory Tracking functionality for at least 3 core product types.

## Objective 2: Scale Local Presence & Reach
**Description:** Automate the technical hurdles of getting discovered locally.

*   **KR 1:** Develop a "Local Presence Optimizer" tool that automatically pushes updates to three major local discovery platforms (Google, Yelp, social media).
*   **KR 2:** Generate 50 unique sample templates for localized promotions within the Community Engagement Toolkit.

## Objective 3: Operational Excellence (Internal)
**Description:** Ensure the "Startup in a Box" itself is a high-functioning model of automation.**

*   **KR 1:** 100% of features mapped to OKRs as verified by project leads.
*   **KR 2:** Establish a robust documentation system for all AI agents involved in the production pipeline.

## Roadmap Focus (Next 3 Months)
- **Month 1:** Foundation & Identity (Vision, Core Architecture).
- **Month 2:** Marketing & Support Automation MVP.
- **Month 3:** Inventory Management & Integration.**
