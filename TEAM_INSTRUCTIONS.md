# Team Instructions for UI Overlays and Ghost AI Focus

## Context
We are currently prioritizing the completion of UI state overlays for "Game Over" and "Level Up" states, as well as finalizing the ghost AI behaviors and visuals. These tasks are critical to achieving our playable core loop and persistent feedback objectives.

## Objectives
- Deliver clear, visually distinct UI overlays for "Game Over" and "Level Up" states that enhance player experience without disrupting game flow.
- Complete implementation and polish of the four distinct ghost AI behaviors: Chase, Ambush, Patrol, and Random.
- Ensure that ghost AI behaviors are visually represented and functionally distinct to provide engaging gameplay.

## Guidelines
- UI overlays should be lightweight, responsive, and consistent with the game's minimalist aesthetic.
- Ghost AI code should be modular and maintainable, allowing easy adjustments and future enhancements.
- Collaborate closely between frontend and backend teams to ensure smooth integration of UI states and AI logic.
- Prioritize performance to maintain 60fps gameplay without UI or AI-induced lag.

## Deliverables
- Fully functional and tested UI overlays for "Game Over" and "Level Up".
- Polished ghost AI behaviors with clear visual cues.
- Documentation of UI overlay implementation and AI behavior logic.

## Timeline
- Target completion within the current development cycle.

Please coordinate progress updates and blockers in the team standups.
