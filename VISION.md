## 🚀 Product Vision: Offline Habit Tracker\n\n**The Core Idea:** A privacy-first, minimal habit tracking application designed to function entirely offline.\n\n### 🎯 Target Users\n*   Privacy-conscious individuals who are wary of cloud services and data harvesting.\n*   Users requiring reliable functionality in areas with poor or no internet connectivity (e.g., remote workers, travelers).\n*   Individuals seeking a simple, distraction-free tool for self-improvement.\n\n### 💡 The Core Problem\nMost modern habit trackers are fundamentally broken by their reliance on the cloud. They force users to trade privacy and reliability for convenience, creating data silos that are difficult to escape. Users need a robust tracking mechanism that treats their personal data as sacred and requires zero external infrastructure to operate.\n\n### ✨ Product Vision Statement\nTo be the most reliable, private, and simple habit tracker available—a digital journal of self-improvement that lives entirely on your device, forever.\\n\n### ✅ Success Metrics (How we measure success)\n1.  **Offline Usage Rate:** High percentage of active users reporting primary usage without internet connectivity.\n2.  **Retention:** Low churn rate driven by the perceived value of privacy and reliability.\n3.  **Cost Efficiency:** Maintaining zero operational expenditure (OpEx) across all development, hosting, and tooling.\n\n### 🛡️ Company Principle: The Zero-Cost Mandate\nThis is non-negotiable. We operate under an absolute **ZERO-COST** mandate. Every decision—from the database choice to the deployment platform—must utilize free and open-source solutions. We will never integrate or propose any paid service, API key, cloud tier, or licensed tool. Our commitment to privacy starts with our financial model.\n"
