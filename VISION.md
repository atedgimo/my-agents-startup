## 🚀 Product Vision: Offline Habit Tracker

**The Vision:** To build the most reliable, privacy-first digital tool for building and tracking personal habits that requires zero internet connectivity to function. We empower users by putting their data entirely under their control, running locally on their device.

---

### 🎯 Target Users
*   **Privacy Advocates:** Individuals highly concerned about data collection and corporate surveillance.
*   **Digital Minimalists/Luddites:** People who prefer simple, reliable tools that don't require constant cloud connectivity or complex setups.
*   **Users in Low-Connectivity Areas:** Anyone who frequently travels or works in environments with unreliable internet access (e.g., remote fieldwork).

### 💡 Core Problem
Existing habit trackers are inherently reliant on the cloud for synchronization and functionality. This creates two major problems:
1.  **Privacy Risk:** Users must trust third parties with sensitive, longitudinal data about their personal behaviors.
2.  **Functionality Gap:** The product fails or degrades when connectivity is poor or non-existent, making it unreliable for core use cases.

### ✅ Success Metrics (North Star)
1.  **Adoption:** Achieving 500 active users within the first quarter who report using the app offline at least once a week.
2.  **Retention:** Maintaining a monthly retention rate above 40%, driven by perceived reliability and privacy benefits.
3.  **Usage Depth:** Average user tracking of 3+ distinct habits over a 30-day period.

---

### 🛡️ Company Principle: The Zero-Cost Mandate (Non-Negotiable)
Our entire operation, product stack, and infrastructure must operate under an absolute **ZERO-COST** mandate. We will never propose, choose, or integrate any paid service, API, cloud tier, or licensed tool. Only free and open-source solutions are permitted. This principle is fundamental to our commitment to user privacy and sustainability.
