# Ideation session

*company week 2, day 1 · 2026-08-02 01:45 · convener: startup-pdm*

The ideation session focused on how to elevate "Chomp" from a functional prototype into a polished arcade experience as specified in **BOARD_BRIEF.md** and **VISION.md**.

### Ideation Summary & Evaluation

I consulted the designer to evaluate several ways to improve the product for our target audience (Casual Players and Retro-Gaming Fans).

| Idea | Description | Arguments vs. Brief/Vision | Decision |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Motion Interpolation** | Decoupling "Logical Position" (grid) from "Visual Position" (Lerp) to ensure smooth movement between tiles. | **Strong Pro:** Directly addresses the "high-quality arcade experience" and "polished" requirements. By keeping collision logic on a fixed grid, we maintain technical simplicity while providing premium feel. | **Keep (v1)** |
| **2. Ghost Trails** | Adding visual trails to ghosts to enhance their presence. | **Minor Pro:** Appeals to retro fans. However, it's an aesthetic "extra" that might slightly increase complexity for the initial release. | **Defer (v2)** |
| **3. Results/Summary State** | Replacing immediate overlays with a dedicated state transition showing scores and high-score comparisons. | **Strong Pro:** Directly supports the requirement of a "high score board." It creates a clear flow from "Action" to "Reflection," making it feel like a finished product rather than a script. | **Keep (v1)** |

### Selected Features for Implementation
I have selected **Motion Interpolation** and **Result Summary States** as the primary improvements. These ensure that we fulfill the quality standards of the vision without adding complex features like multi-level support or sound effects prematurely.
