## Outcome

The ideation session for **Chomp** was conducted to identify high-impact enhancements that improve the user experience for both **Casual Players** and **Retro-Gaming Fans** while strictly adhering to the "No Audio" and "One Maze" constraints of `BOARD_BRIEF.md`.

### Ideation & Analysis
The core challenge identified during the session with the designer was translating high-quality "game feel" into a purely visual medium because audio is restricted in v1. We focused on three pillars:
1.  **Visual Differentiation:** Ensuring that distinct mechanics (the four ghost types) are visually obvious to players.
2.  **State Feedback:** Providing clear visual cues for when the player is in "power-up" mode or successfully interacts with the environment.
3.  **Reward Cycles:** Enhancing the win/loss screens to reinforce the core loop of competing for high scores on the local leaderboard.

### Proposed & Selected Features
The following features were selected because they maximize "juice" and clarity without adding new complexity:

*   **Ghost Identity & State Visuals**: Assigning distinct colors to each ghost behavior (Chase, Ambush, Patrol, Random) and a visual state change when `power_up` is active. This fulfills the retro-fan requirement for distinct behaviors.
*   **Visual Juice & Feedback Mechanisms**: Adding "pulse" effects on pellet collection and an aura around the player during power-ups to replace the missing audio cues.
*   **Enhanced Game State Overlays**: Improving the visual presentation of `WON` and `LOST` states to highlight score persistence, appealing to the casual user's desire for a satisfying end to their session.
